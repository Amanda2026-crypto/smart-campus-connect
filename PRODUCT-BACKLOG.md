## Prioritized Product Backlog

| Priority | Story ID | User Story | Story Points | Justification |
|----------|----------|------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | US-001 | As a **student**, I want to **register using my CPUT email address** so that **I can create an account and access the system**. | 3 | Without registration, users cannot access the system. This is the foundation for everything else. |
| 2 | US-002 | As a **student**, I want to **log in with my email and password** so that **I can securely access my student dashboard**. | 2 | Users need to authenticate after registration. High business value. |
| 3 | US-003 | As a **faculty member**, I want to **log in with my staff email and password** so that **I can access my course management dashboard**. | 2 | Faculty need access to manage courses. Equal priority to student login. |
| 4 | US-004 | As a **student**, I want to **view my enrolled courses** so that **I can see my class schedule and academic progress**. | 3 | Core academic feature. Students need to see their courses first. |
| 5 | US-005 | As a **student**, I want to **view my grades for each course** so that **I can track my academic performance**. | 3 | Students track performance. High demand feature. |
| 6 | US-009 | As a **student**, I want to **find available study rooms on campus** so that **I don't waste time walking to rooms that are already full**. | 5 | High student pain point. Wasted time finding spaces. Directly addresses stakeholder concern. |
| 7 | US-010 | As a **student**, I want to **book a study room for a specific time slot** so that **I can guarantee a space to study**. | 3 | Natural follow-up to finding rooms. Completes the feature. |
| 8 | US-012 | As a **student**, I want to **browse and register for campus events** so that **I don't miss activities I'm interested in**. | 3 | Students miss events due to poor discovery. High value. |
| 9 | US-006 | As a **student**, I want to **submit assignments online** so that **I don't have to print and hand in physical papers**. | 5 | Eliminates paper submissions. Faculty and student benefit. |
| 10 | US-007 | As a **faculty member**, I want to **create and publish assignments** so that **students can submit their work digitally**. | 5 | Faculty need to create assignments before students can submit. |
| 11 | US-008 | As a **faculty member**, I want to **take attendance using QR codes** so that **I can save time and avoid manual errors**. | 5 | Faculty pain point. Manual attendance takes class time. |
| 12 | US-011 | As a **student**, I want to **track campus shuttles in real-time** so that **I can plan my commute and avoid waiting**. | 5 | Students waste time waiting for shuttles. High frustration. |
| 13 | US-013 | As a **student**, I want to **create study groups for my courses** so that **I can collaborate with classmates**. | 5 | Social feature. Helps students collaborate. |
| 14 | US-014 | As a **student**, I want to **send messages in my study groups** so that **I can discuss coursework and share resources**. | 3 | Enables group communication. Follows group creation. |
| 15 | US-015 | As a **faculty member**, I want to **create events for my course** so that **I can notify students about guest lectures or deadlines**. | 3 | Faculty need to create events. |
| 16 | US-016 | As an **administrator**, I want to **approve or reject event submissions** so that **only appropriate events are published**. | 3 | Admin oversight needed for event quality. |
| 17 | US-017 | As a **student**, I want to **view my meal plan balance** so that **I know how many swipes I have left**. | 2 | Useful for students with meal plans. Lower priority. |
| 18 | US-018 | As a **student**, I want to **report a lost item** so that **I can increase my chances of getting it back**. | 2 | Nice to have. Not critical for MVP. |
| 19 | US-019 | As a **system admin**, I want to **send emergency alerts to all users** so that **students and staff receive critical safety information**. | 3 | Important but can be added after core features work. |
